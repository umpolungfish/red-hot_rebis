# CLINK Universal Detox Gland — Organoid Assembly Protocol
## Tier: O₂ (critical self-regulating biological system)

### Day 0: Cell Preparation
1. Thaw HEK293T Sensor cells (passage <15) in DMEM + 10% FBS
2. Thaw HEK293T Producer cells (passage <15) in DMEM + 10% FBS
3. Thaw HUVEC Support cells (passage <8) in EGM-2 medium
4. Count and assess viability (>90% required)

### Day 1: Microencapsulation
1. Mix cells at ratio: 5% Sensor : 90% Producer : 5% Support
2. Total cell number: 5 × 10⁶ cells per gland
3. Resuspend in 2% sodium alginate in PBS
4. Extrude through 22G needle into 100 mM CaCl₂ crosslinking bath
5. Wash with PBS, coat with 0.1% poly-L-ornithine (30 min)
6. Second alginate coating (1%, 10 min)
7. PEG grafting: 10 mM mPEG-NHS in 50 mM HEPES, pH 8.0 (30 min)

### Day 3-7: Maturation in Bioreactor
1. Transfer encapsulated cells to rotating wall vessel bioreactor
2. Medium: DMEM/F12 + 10% FBS + 1% Pen/Strep
3. Perfusion rate: 0.5 mL/min, 37°C, 5% CO₂
4. Monitor glucose consumption and lactate production daily
5. Day 7: Assess fusion protein production by ELISA

### Day 7-14: Vascularization
1. Embed mature organoid in hyaluronic acid/collagen hydrogel
2. Incorporate sacrificial microchannels (200 μm Pluronic F127 template)
3. Template removal by cooling to 4°C for 15 min
4. Seed HUVECs into channels at 2 × 10⁶ cells/mL
5. Perfuse with EGM-2 + 50 ng/mL VEGF at 0.2 mL/min

### Day 21: Quality Control
1. Viability: Live/Dead assay (>85% viable required)
2. Barrier function: FITC-dextran exclusion (100 kDa cutoff)
3. Toxin challenge: Test with 10 μM paraoxon, measure antidote secretion
4. Sterility: Gram stain, mycoplasma PCR, endotoxin < 0.5 EU/mL
