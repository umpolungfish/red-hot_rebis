precursor lattice and both enrichment tests failed anywhere else. They name the
package now.

parenthesised import, and two of its methods written bottom to top. Restored from the
last revision that parses, with the import repaired.

longer does because the patches were applied long ago. Finding its own result in
place is a patch having worked; both scripts say so and stop, instead of failing.
