rebis.sidechain --pdb crashed on every real structure with KeyError: '⊡'. Every one of sidechain_algebra.py's 20 sidechain tuples and 4 environment tuples stored its twelfth field under "◻", a plain white square, instead of "⊡", the real Protection mark — crystal_navigator's tensor/meet/join iterate the canonical twelve marks, which use the real one, so a[p]/b[p] found nothing for that key and raised.

Fixed at the source: sed across sidechain_algebra.py's own dict literals and its one direct read of that field, "◻" to "⊡", 25 occurrences. Verified against the actual instrument: all 80 sidechain-times-environment pairs now run without error, and rebis.sidechain --pdb 6UPT — the exact command that crashed — runs clean end to end on all 239 residues of a real fetched structure, tiers and frustration ranking included.

"◻" is still this repo's own convention in several other files (ch3mpiler_serpentrod_pipeline.py, gene_to_protein_pipeline.py, genetic_tuples.py, ligand_from_active_site.py, serpent_rod_v2.py, dual_link_sicpovm.py) — self-consistent within each of those, and none of them hand a dict across the crystal_navigator boundary the way sidechain_algebra.py does, so none of them are broken by this. Left untouched; not this bug.
