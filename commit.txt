Four commands named classes that do not exist

The forge command asked for a class named after the forge; the forge is driven by
the Eagle Cycle and its entry point is the simulation that runs it. The Casimir
command asked for a designer; the cavity is built from its parameters, and the target
gap sets the plate separation. Both run.

The telomere analysis built a cell without the identifier its type requires, then
without any telomeres in it, so the signalling step reduced over an empty array and
reported it as an error inside otherwise complete output. It now fills the cell the
way the simulation does, one cell carrying its full complement at baseline length,
and the ATM cascade reports activity, phosphorylation and the p53 arm.

The facades exported the names that do not exist alongside the ones that do; they
export what is there.
