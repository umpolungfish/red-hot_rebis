Every tuple in the toolchain now addresses the marks

The alphabet moved and most of this repo was left addressing the letters it retired.
Sixty files carried tuple keys of the old form, so anything that composed, joined or
measured a distance across a module boundary either crashed on a missing key or
silently computed over misses. CLINK could not walk its own chain. The sidechain
algebra crashed on the first tensor. The gene imscriber emitted tuples nothing else
could read.

All of them rekeyed to ⊢ ⊣ ≻ ≺ ⋈ ⊤ ∈ ∋ ⊙ ⊥ ⊞ ◻, and the chain now walks L0 to L8 with
the tiers ascending to O_∞ as its own documentation claims.

The ligand facade exposed nothing at all, alone among the domains, so the census read
zero symbols for it while the engines underneath imported fine. It exposes them now.
Given lysozyme and its catalytic pair, the designer returns glycosides carrying a
carboxylate, which is the peptidoglycan chemistry lysozyme actually cleaves.
