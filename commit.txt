Correction: the enclosure must be work, not any mark

The enclosure law was stated as "encloses at least one mark", which is too weak.
Seven of the twelve do work — ≻ ≺ ⋈ ⊤ ⊥ ⊞ ◻ — and five do not: the boundaries ⊢ ⊣,
the self-reference ⊙, and fork and fuse themselves. ⊢∈⊙∋⊣ reads N on both engines,
as do ⊢∈⊙⊙⊙∋⊣ and ⊢∈⊣∋⊣, while ⊢∈⋈∋⊣ and ⊢∈◻∋⊣ read T.

At balance: T if some ∈…∋ pair encloses at least one of the seven work marks, else
N. Still 65 of 65 over the sweep — no word in the corpus separates the readings, so
the corpus could not have caught it; ⊢∈⊙∋⊣ is the witness.

A fork enclosing only self-reference has looked at itself and returned, which is N
by the same argument as the empty loop.
