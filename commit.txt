Manuscript: The Fold Verdict of a Coding Sequence

The four-valued readout stated in closed form and proved from the cyclic pairing
definition, then carried through the genetic code to two codon families.

Lemma (Surplus): at most one of the unmatched sets is non-empty and their signed
difference is the fork count minus the fuse count — so B and F cannot coexist, the
surplus species is fixed by a sign, and the magnitude is invisible to the readout.

Theorem (Closed form): B when d > 0, F when d < 0, and at d = 0, T if some paired
region contains one of the seven work marks, else N.

Theorem (Readout of a coding sequence), the carrying result: count AAY against CAR
in the frame. AAY > CAR is B, CAR > AAY is F, equal closes T exactly when some AAY
is separated from its paired CAR by a Cys, Tyr, Phe, Ile, Asp, Lys or Glu codon,
and is N otherwise. Nothing else about the sequence affects the value.

The enclosure condition is separated from the weaker non-emptiness reading by an
explicit witness rather than by the data — the corpus cannot tell them apart.
Verified on sixty-five deposited structures and on nine reconstructed transcripts.
Position states plainly that no correspondence to any thermodynamic or kinetic
observable is claimed.

Eight pages, builds clean.
