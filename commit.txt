The migration I said was complete was not

I swept for quoted retired letters and reported that every tuple in the toolchain
addresses the marks. That covered what I had grepped for. Files keyed by the ASCII
axis abbreviations D T R P F K G Gm Ph H S W, with values written as English names
like tri, boxtimes, sub and hetero, were invisible to it, and there were many.

Every one is carried now, key and value together, by ordinal so the conversion is
exact: the seven stages of the gene pipeline, the genetic tuple defaults, the site
designer, the antibody designer, the validators and writers, the organism designs,
the stress tests and the analysis scripts. The gene pipeline now reports its stages
in the marks with Shavian values, dna_gene through quaternary_structure, each with
its B4 face and its Frobenius verdict.

Two faults were hidden behind the old labels. The stage tuples wrote "two", "one" and
"zero" on axes whose ordinal table is keyed 0, 1, 2, inf, so those lookups silently
missed. And the site designer's blend rules named the short forms, so once the axes
were marks nothing matched and the fused ordinal was never assigned; the branch that
should have caught it did not exist, so an unnamed axis left the variable unbound
instead of saying so. Both fixed, and the rules now say what each group of axes does.

The site designer also carried the residue assignment a fifth time, with asparagine,
glutamine and histidine in the wrong places. It reads V⊙x now, like the rest.
