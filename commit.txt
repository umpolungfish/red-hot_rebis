The chain's last stage reports what its engine found

The retrosynthetic stage read two keys the plan does not carry, so it printed no
disconnections and no cycles for a target whose plan had found eleven sites and ten
disconnections. It reads the count, the ranked sites and the Frobenius score the plan
actually returns, and says whether the route re-forms.

The therapy pipeline defaulted its key to an empty string with no choices, so running
it bare threw on the empty key. The choices come from the therapies the pipeline
defines, and an omitted key runs the first.

The gene pipeline command built its pipeline with no sequence and passed the sequence
to run(), which takes the subunit count. The sequence goes where it belongs and the
seven stages complete.
