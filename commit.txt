The Dimensionality family had lost its names, and two modules printed with helpers they never imported

An earlier pass stripped the underscored names from the Dimensionality family alone
and left the other eleven as they were, so the family index, which splits a name on
its separator, read the values themselves as families. Twelve families became fifteen
of the wrong kind and every closure distance died on a missing key. The names are
back in the shape the other eleven use, every family prefix now names the mark it
addresses, and the generators point at the names rather than at bare values. Twelve
families, cardinalities four five four five three five three four five four three
four.

Two modules called the printing helpers while their only import of them sat inside a
template string for generated code, so the generated stubs could print and the
modules themselves could not.

Sibling modules inside the kernel package imported each other by bare name, which
works from inside the directory and nowhere else. Package-qualified now.
