# Composition before order: the primary fold diagnostic

s590 is unrepairable not because its forks and fuses are misordered but
because its marks are anchor-saturated — ⊢ 8, ⊣ 9, ⋈ 7 against one ≻ of
forward work (01). Fork/fuse balance is a second-order property; the first
question is whether the mark census admits a fold at all — enough work
between the boundaries to carry any fork to its fuse.

The T-closers from the sweep had balanced work; the anchor-saturated ones
could not close under any rearrangement.
