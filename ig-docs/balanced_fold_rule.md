The balanced-template ob3ect (3db50dda, grounded full, Lean-verified) answered the

## What each division pairs with

The motif is ∈ ≻ ⊤ ≺ ⊥ ∋ ⋈ : split, then BOTH arms worked (forward ≻, true ⊤,
reverse ≺, false ⊥), then fuse, then link. Every ∈ carries its own ∋ one motif
later, with a full both-arm evaluation between them. A division pairs with its own
merge, and the merge only fires after both arms have done work — which is exactly
what the failing variants lacked: their surplus fuses fired inert, merging arms
that were never split or never worked.

## How balance is held as the structure grows

By repetition of the balanced unit. Growth adds paired ∈/∋ motifs, so the
fork/fuse count stays equal at every size — a 1:1 participation ratio, which the
ob3ect types as ⊞=𐑙, the self-referential stoichiometry limit (the grammar IS the
measured system). Balance is structural, held by construction, not detected after
the fact.
